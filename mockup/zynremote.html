Piano keyboard

Show / hide the pianoSpace
Octave down / up← →
Lower row of notesZ X C … /
Upper row of notesQ W E … P

The piano plays with the computer keyboard or the mouse / touch (slide across keys). Each row is one and a half octaves starting at C of the current octave; the keys above each row (S D G… and 2 3 5…) are the black keys. The arrows (or the − / + buttons) shift the octave, and the slider sets the velocity. Notes are sent to the MIDI channel of the active chain.
